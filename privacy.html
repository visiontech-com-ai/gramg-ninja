Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026 · Applies to the GramG Ninja browser extension for Chrome and Edge.
In short: GramG Ninja runs entirely inside your browser. It does not send your data to us or to any third-party server, has no analytics or tracking, shows no ads, and does not sell or share your data. Everything you enter or process stays on your device.

1. Who we are

GramG Ninja is a free productivity extension published by Vision Technologies and Robotics. It helps authorized users of the Government of India / State Government VB-G RAM G (Viksit Bharat – Rozgar & Aajeevika Mission, Gramin / MGNREGA) portal (*.dord.gov.in) complete routine data-entry and reporting tasks faster. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any government body.

2. What the extension stores — and where

All storage is local to your browser/device. The extension has no backend and no account system.

3. Chrome account sync

Your signing details may be saved with chrome.storage.sync so they survive a reinstall and follow you to the same Chrome profile on another computer. If you are signed in to Chrome, Google mirrors this small amount of data through your own Google account; that transfer is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, not by us, and we never receive it. If you are not signed in to Chrome, the data stays only on your device.

4. What the extension does NOT do

  • It does not send your data to Vision Technologies and Robotics or to any third-party server.
  • It contains no analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not read or collect data from websites other than when you explicitly act (see §6).
  • It does not run remote or hosted code — all libraries (pdf.js, jsPDF, SheetJS) are bundled and run offline.

5. Sensitive / third-party data

The caste-wise wage breakup uses caste values that already exist in the portal's public Registration Application Register, which you as an authorized officer access on the portal. GramG Ninja only caches this locally on your device to compute totals; it is never uploaded anywhere. You can clear it at any time (see §8). Please use this feature only for legitimate, authorized administrative work.

7. Data retention

Data stays until you remove it: uninstalling the extension removes its chrome.storage data, and clearing the site's browser data removes the portal localStorage. Synced signing details are also removed from your Google account when Chrome next syncs after uninstall.

8. How to delete your data

  • Signing details, settings, caste cache: open the extension's options and clear the fields, or remove the extension from chrome://extensions.
  • Work Demand progress: use Start over in the Work Demand panel, or clear site data for the portal.

9. Children

GramG Ninja is a workplace tool for government administrative staff and is not directed to children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the extension's release notes.
