Welcome to GramG Ninja

Your toolkit for the VB-G RAM G (MGNREGA) portal — here's what you can do.

Every feature runs right inside the portal pages you already use. Open the relevant page and a panel appears automatically.

📝

Certificate Auto-Fill

On the Work Entry page, attach the certificate PDFs and fill each certificate block with your saved signing-authority details in one click. Set them once in the toolbar popup.

📋

Work Demand Automation

On the Work Demand page, load an Excel of demands and the extension enters them for you — per-applicant status, resume/stop any time, and an auto-exported results sheet.

🗂️

Work Allocation Automation NEW

On the Work Allocation page, load an Excel and the extension selects each work code, ticks the matching applicants, fills the allocation dates, and saves — skipping and logging anything that can't be allocated.

📊

Data Extraction

Pull any table or grid from the page you're viewing and download it as CSV or JSON — or consolidate tables across several open tabs into one report.

🔧

Portal Helpers (Misc tab)

Show passwords as you type and re-enable copy & paste on portal fields that block it. Both are on by default — turn them off in the popup's Misc tab.

👁️
Keep a human in the loop. The extension speeds up data entry, but it doesn't replace your judgement. Always review the results sheet and the portal before treating a batch as final.
